# Design Studio promo: narration script

The video ships with on-screen titles and music only. This is the script for a voiceover if you add one
(for example with ElevenLabs, as in https://github.com/AKCodez/promo-video-skill: generate one clip per line, then drop the
files in `public/vo/` and add an `<Audio>` per scene in `src/Film.tsx`). Timings are approximate.

0:00  AI agents can design. But who is in charge? You are.
0:07  **Direct a team.** Write one brief. Claude Code and Codex each design it, independently, without seeing each other's work.
0:16  **Experiment freely.** Spread the agents in different directions. Run with your rules on, and again with them off. Try different guideline profiles.
0:28  **Watch them work.** See what each agent is doing, how long it has taken, and what it costs, while it happens.
0:38  **See what your rules cost.** Compare the same idea with and without the rules, and keep the breaks you like.
0:48  **Bring your own guidelines.** Point at your DESIGN.md, rules and tokens. Nothing to paste. Review, then save a version, or branch a new profile to test a different set of rules.
1:02  **Point, don't prompt.** Pin feedback on the screen. Only that part is revised, and the studio checks nothing else changed.
1:12  **Audit on demand.** Accessibility checks and your own legal and design-review documents, run when you decide. Anything an agent only thinks is clearly labelled unverified.
1:26  **Decide with a paper trail.** Approve with a reason, set aside with a reason, and get a decision log you can share.
1:36  **And take it with you.** It runs on your computer and works on your phone, on the same Wi-Fi.
1:44  Design Studio. Direct AI agents. Keep the judgment.
